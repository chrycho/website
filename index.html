01 / ABOUT ME

I am a Brazilian astronomer and postdoctoral researcher at the Observatório Nacional in Rio de Janeiro. I combine observational campaigns, photometry and light-curve modelling of stellar occultations to study Centaurs, trans-Neptunian objects and their surroundings.

I led international studies that discovered Quaoar’s second ring and first observed Chiron’s evolving ring system. My doctoral work earned an honourable mention from the Brazilian Astronomical Society (SAB), and I received a FAPERJ Nota 10 fellowship. The International Astronomical Union named asteroid (20276) Chrystianpereira in recognition of my contributions to planetary astronomy.
